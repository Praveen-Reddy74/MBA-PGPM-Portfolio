# Statistical Methods for Decision Making — Adani Ports & SEZ Analysis

**Term:** 1 · **Subject:** SMDM — Statistical Methods for Decision Making · **Professor:** Prof. Prasad Patil (per subject workbook)
**Type:** Group project (statistical analysis report + deck) · **Status:** Group Project — 6 members

## Objective
Apply statistical decision-making tools — EDA, regression, forecasting, clustering, and hypothesis testing — to real stock-market data for Adani Ports & SEZ Ltd., and connect the techniques to functional business use cases.

## Approach
- Descriptive analysis of Adani Ports stock (mean/median close price, average daily volume, deliverable percentage, standard deviation).
- Mapped statistical techniques to functional business areas: clustering and A/B testing for marketing (customer classification, trend prediction), logistic regression and correlation for HR (attrition prediction, training-impact analysis), and control charts/forecasting for operations.
- Applied regression, forecasting, and clustering techniques to the stock dataset as worked examples.

## Output
A written project report and an accompanying presentation deck.

## What this demonstrates
Bridges foundational statistics with applied business decision-making — showing how the same toolkit (regression, clustering, hypothesis testing) applies across marketing, HR, and operations functions.

## Academic context
Graded group coursework for Term 1's Statistical Methods for Decision Making course.
