# Market Analytics for a Quick Commerce Business

**Term:** 5 · **Subject:** MRA — Marketing and Retail Analysis · **Professor:** Prof. Rajarshi Pandit (per subject workbook)
**Type:** Group project (analytics report) · **Status:** Group Project — members not enumerated in the extracted report text

## Objective
Evaluate business performance, customer behavior, delivery efficiency, and churn risk for a Quick Commerce (Q-Commerce) business, and derive actionable insights to improve retention and revenue stability.

## Context
Quick Commerce businesses compete primarily on delivery speed, repeat purchase behavior, and customer retention, operating in a highly competitive daily-use goods (groceries/FMCG) segment.

## Approach
- Performed Python-based exploratory data analysis and data transformation on transactional and customer-level data.
- Identified key business problems: customer inactivity/churn risk, delivery delays affecting satisfaction, declining engagement versus historical activity, and revenue concentration risk (limited products/brands/geographies).
- Built supporting visualizations and dashboards in Power BI.

## Methods / Tools
Python (Jupyter Notebook) for EDA/transformation, Power BI for dashboarding.

## Output
A written analytics report covering business overview, problem identification, and solution approach.

## What this demonstrates
End-to-end retail/e-commerce analytics — from raw transactional data to a churn/retention diagnosis and a dashboard-ready set of business questions.

## Academic context
Graded group coursework for Term 5's Marketing and Retail Analysis course.
