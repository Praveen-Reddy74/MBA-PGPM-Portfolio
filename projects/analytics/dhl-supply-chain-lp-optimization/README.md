# DHL Supply Chain — Shipping Cost Optimization (Linear Programming)

**Term:** 2 · **Subject:** QMDM — Quantitative Methods for Decision Making · **Professor:** Dr. Suryaprakash (per subject workbook)
**Type:** Group project (quantitative modeling) · **Status:** Group Project — 5 members

## Objective
Minimize DHL's TV-shipment shipping costs across a multi-node logistics network while staying within a fixed budget and meeting delivery constraints.

## Approach
- Formulated a linear-programming model with 64 decision variables covering shipment routes between origin/destination nodes (ODMs) and two product types (32" and 42" LCD TVs).
- Modeled constraints for demand fulfillment (920,000 units of 42" LCDs, 530,000 units of 32" LCDs), a CNY 3 billion total budget, production capacity, route availability, weight limits, and CO2 emissions.
- Solved the optimization model using Lingo.

## Output
A presentation covering problem scoping, the network diagram, the mathematical model formulation, and the optimized shipping-cost output.

## What this demonstrates
Applied operations-research / linear-programming skills on a realistic, multi-constraint logistics optimization problem.

## Academic context
Graded group coursework for Term 2's Quantitative Methods for Decision Making course.
