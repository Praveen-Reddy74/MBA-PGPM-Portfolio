## Objective
Develop a project-management execution plan for the Go-To-Market launch of Samsung Ballie in India, targeting a festival-season (Diwali) launch window.

## Approach
Plans a hybrid project-management approach — Waterfall for fixed-timeline distribution/logistics workstreams, Agile for dynamic digital-marketing and software-localization workstreams — over a stated ₹4,00,00,000 budget and 6-month timeline, including critical-path planning, risk mitigation, and earned-value tracking.

## Output
A comprehensive written PM report covering methodology choice, budget, timeline, and risk management.

## What this demonstrates
Applies hybrid Waterfall/Agile project-management methodology to a realistic consumer-technology product launch, with attention to critical-path and risk management.
