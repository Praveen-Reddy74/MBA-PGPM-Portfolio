# ERP & CRM Implementation for ITC Limited

**Term:** 3 · **Subject:** ERP — Enterprise Resource Planning · **Professor:** Prof. Vinit Thakur (confirmed in the original report; matches subject workbook) · Assisted by: Mr. Hasan Haider Jafri (TA)
**Type:** Group project (coursework report) · **Status:** Group Project — 5 members

## Objective
Analyze how ERP and CRM systems could serve as the digital backbone for a large, diversified conglomerate — ITC Limited — across its FMCG, Agri-Business, Paperboards & Packaging, IT, and Hotels divisions.

## Context
ITC's business spans B2C (FMCG), B2B (Packaging), and complex agricultural sourcing (Agri-Business/e-Choupal network), making integrated ERP/CRM design a genuinely cross-functional systems problem.

## Approach
Applied ERP and CRM concepts across four functional areas: supply-chain and logistics integration (farmer-to-retail traceability), financial and compliance management (consolidated multi-division reporting), manufacturing and production planning (demand-aligned scheduling), and human-resource management (workforce data for 36,000+ employees).

## Output
A written report mapping ERP/CRM concepts to concrete ITC business scenarios, with worked examples for each functional area.

## What this demonstrates
Systems-thinking applied to enterprise architecture for a large, multi-division real-world company — connecting IT-systems concepts to operational and financial business outcomes.

## Academic context
Graded group coursework for Term 3's Enterprise Resource Planning course.
