# Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max — Positioning Strategy

**Term:** 1 · **Subject:** MM1 — Marketing Management 1 · **Professor:** Prof. Parveen Ahluwalia (per subject workbook)
**Type:** Group project (marketing analysis) · **Status:** Group Project — "Group 3"; individual members not named in the extracted slide text

## Objective
Analyze consumer trends and positioning strategy for the Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max in the Indian market.

## Approach
- Identified key consumer trends: feel-over-features purchasing behavior, product sophistication expectations, quicker device-switching cycles, and the importance of in-store experience.
- Defined the target audience by demographics, income, education, profession, geography, and psychographics (SEC class A1).
- Developed a brand positioning statement ("Class, in the palm of your hand") with supporting proof points.
- Applied Ansoff's Matrix to evaluate Apple India's growth strategy options (market penetration, product development, market development, diversification).

## Output
A presentation deck covering consumer trends, target audience definition, positioning statement, and strategic growth-matrix analysis.

## What this demonstrates
Classic marketing-strategy frameworks (STP, positioning statements, Ansoff's Matrix) applied to a real, contemporary product launch.

## Academic context
Graded group coursework for Term 1's Marketing Management 1 course.
