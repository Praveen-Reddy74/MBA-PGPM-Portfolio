# Go-To-Market Strategy for Samsung Ballie in India

**Term:** 2 · **Subject:** MM II — Marketing Management II · **Professor:** Dr. Shailaja Manocha (per subject workbook)
**Type:** Group project — **authorship not fully confirmed** (slide deck lists "Group Number: 01"; the extracted slide text surfaced only one name, which is not strong enough evidence to call this an individual project)

## Objective
Develop a go-to-market strategy for launching Samsung's Ballie — an AI-powered home companion robot — in the Indian market.

## Approach
Covers the full GTM framework: executive summary, product introduction, PESTLE macro-environment analysis, market needs and trends, market sizing, SWOT, competitive analysis, a perceptual positioning map, target market (STP), the marketing mix (4Ps), and strategic frameworks (BCG Matrix, Ansoff's Matrix). Also addresses competitive responses, customer/collaborator incentive design, and pricing strategy (premium skimming), with year-1/year-2 unit-sales and revenue targets.

## Output
A comprehensive marketing-strategy presentation deck.

## What this demonstrates
End-to-end GTM strategy development for a real, publicly-announced product concept — market sizing, competitive positioning, and go-to-market tactics grounded in a plausible Indian market context.
