# AR Try-Before-You-Buy — Purchase Intent Study (L'Oréal & Garnier)

**Term:** 6 · **Subject:** SC — "Spatial Computing" (workbook lists this as "Saptial Computing" — a typo in the source workbook; see academic index) · **Professor:** Prof. Harish Kumar (per subject workbook)
**Type:** Group project (quantitative experimental research) · **Status:** "SC Group 2"; individual members not enumerated in the extracted content

## Objective
Study the impact of Augmented Reality (AR) virtual try-on tools on purchase intention, confidence, and consumer engagement, using L'Oréal and Garnier hair-color AR try-on tools as the experimental stimulus.

## Research Question
Does AR virtual try-on improve engagement, confidence, and purchase intention compared to traditional product viewing?

## Approach
- Quantitative experimental study design.
- Participants (N = 41, target group 18–30+, smartphone users) used AR hair-color try-on tools from L'Oréal and Garnier.
- Measured dependent variables: purchase intention, consumer engagement, product understanding, confidence in decision-making, and perceived realism.

## Output
A written/visual research report presenting the introduction, research problem, research question, methodology, and sample design.

## What this demonstrates
Applied consumer-behavior research design — a real experimental study (not just a survey) measuring how an emerging retail technology (AR try-on) affects purchase decisions.

## Academic context
Graded group coursework for Term 6's Spatial Computing course.
