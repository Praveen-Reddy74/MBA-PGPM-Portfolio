# Financial Risk Analytics — Banking Sector Risk Framework

**Term:** 6 · **Subject:** FRA — Financial Risk Analytics · **Professor:** Dr. Bappaditya Mukhopadhyay (confirmed in the original report; matches subject workbook)
**Type:** Group project (coursework report) · **Status:** Group Project — 6 members

## Objective
Develop a simplified risk-management framework for a bank lending to two contrasting sectors of the economy — Infrastructure and Pharmaceuticals — covering risk identification, probability estimation, and loss estimation.

## Approach
- Selected Infrastructure (large investments, long completion periods, exposure to delays/regulatory change/cost overruns) and Pharmaceuticals (innovation-driven, regulatory-approval and R&D risk) as contrasting risk environments.
- Framework evaluated three risk categories per sector: financial risk, operational risk, and climate/regulatory risk.
- Built a probability matrix (p_ij) and loss-estimation matrix (L_ij) per sector, then combined them into expected-loss calculations.
- Compared overall risk exposure across the two sectors and proposed risk-management strategies.

## Output
A written report covering the full framework: risk identification, probability/loss matrices, expected-loss calculations, sector comparison, and mitigation strategies.

## What this demonstrates
Structured, quantitative approach to credit/sector risk assessment in banking — translating qualitative risk categories into a probability × loss expected-value framework.

## Academic context
Graded group coursework for Term 6's Financial Risk Analytics course.
