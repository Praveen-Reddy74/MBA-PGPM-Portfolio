# Agilus Diagnostics — Profit Sensitivity & Operating Leverage Study

**Term:** 2 · **Subject:** MA — Managerial Accounting · **Professor:** Dr. Kirti Sharma (per subject workbook)
**Type:** **Individual assignment** (explicitly labeled "MBA Individual Assignment" in the original submission) · **Author:** M Praveen Kumar

## Objective
Perform a Cost-Volume-Profit (CVP) analysis of Agilus Diagnostics (formerly SRL Diagnostics) to understand how changes in sales, variable costs, and fixed costs affect profitability, and to recommend operating-leverage optimization strategies.

## Context
Agilus Diagnostics has transformed into one of India's largest diagnostic networks through investment in AI, genomics, digital health, and automation — a real, contemporary company used as the analysis subject.

## Approach
- Cost structure analysis and cost segregation into fixed and variable components.
- Base-year CVP analysis, followed by three scenario analyses: a sales increase, a variable-cost increase, and a fixed-cost increase.
- Profit sensitivity analysis and Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL) calculation.
- Break-even analysis and strategic recommendations for operating-leverage optimization.

## Output
A full written report: executive summary, company overview, cost analysis, three scenario analyses, sensitivity analysis, DOL, break-even analysis, and recommendations.

## What this demonstrates
Rigorous, individually-authored managerial accounting analysis — CVP modeling and operating-leverage reasoning applied to a real company's cost structure.
