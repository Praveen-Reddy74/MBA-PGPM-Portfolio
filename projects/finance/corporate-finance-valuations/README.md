# Corporate Finance — Cost of Capital & Valuation (Blue Star, Kalyan Jewellers, Asian Paints)

**Term:** 3 · **Subject:** CF-1 — Corporate Finance-1 · **Professor:** Dr. Smita Mazumdar (per subject workbook)
**Type:** Group project (coursework report + models) · **Status:** Group Project — 6 members

## Objective
Estimate the cost of equity, cost of debt, and WACC for three listed Indian companies (sector: Consumer Durables) using CAPM and market data, as a basis for valuation.

## Approach
- Cost of Equity via CAPM (Ke = Rf + β×(Rm − Rf)), using the 10-year G-Sec yield as the risk-free rate, company-specific beta, and an assumed equity market risk premium.
- Cost of Debt from each company's reported interest expense and total debt, adjusted for the effective tax rate.
- WACC computed by weighting cost of equity and after-tax cost of debt by market value of equity and book value of debt.
- Company-specific inputs (beta, debt, market cap, interest expense, tax rate) sourced from public financial-data platforms (cited in the report as Screener.in).

## Output
A written report (methodology, assumptions, and calculations for all three companies) plus three per-company Excel valuation workbooks (`CF1_Blue Star.xlsx`, `CF1 Kalyan Jewellers.xlsx`, `CF1_Asian Paints.xlsx`), included here unmodified as they contain no personal or classmate information.

## What this demonstrates
Applied corporate-finance fundamentals — CAPM, cost of debt, and WACC — grounded in real, sourced market data rather than illustrative numbers.
